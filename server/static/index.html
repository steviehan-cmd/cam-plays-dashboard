Play guide

The 12 plays. Each drawing is one ticker's ladder, S6 at the bottom to R6 at the top.

HH days today's CPR higher than yesterday's
LL days today's CPR lower than yesterday's
With the CPR filter on: H days allow only H plays and L days only L plays (a neutral day allows both). A narrow CPR allows only plays that trade outwards (B, C, D); a wide CPR only plays that trade inwards (A, E). F plays are allowed at any width. A play enters when a bar touches the entry pivot and ends at the target, or fails when a bar closes back beyond the entry pivot.
